# Accounted-cell batch contract (producer ingress implemented; downstream pending)

The frozen membership remains 25 companies and exactly 250 distinct category cells per batch (80 batches/20,000 cells). **Accounted** means every canonical cell has an explicit truthful disposition; it does not mean research was performed, a benefit found, or terminal absence proven. Approval of a batch would authorize only independently reviewed atomic positive propositions. `not_researched` means no request was made, has zero attempts and no evidence, closure, positive, or absence claim. `research_incomplete` means at least one request was attempted and remains unresolved. `source_access_failed` requires a failed request. `conflicting_evidence` requires two distinct source-bearing successful attempt receipts and distinct capture hashes/locators, plus a contradictory source marker; semantic opposition still needs independent review of actual bytes. `not_publicly_verifiable` remains reserved for independently proven `no_public_info` closure with all required routes, retained destination evidence, and adjudicated leads. These states are not benefit counts.

**Authority boundary:** The mixed candidate fixture is a structural ingress test only. Its source and capture hashes are test-double claims without corresponding bytes in the candidate inventory; the candidate compiler validates schema, receipt content hashes and source IDs, **not** source or capture contents. The controller's artifact inventory has real file bytes and binds source→attempt→capture for every researched disposition; a `not_researched` disposition correctly has no artifacts and explicitly false closure. Do not call mixed candidate fixtures retained evidence, or infer source contents from a hash/HTTPS capture locator. No release or approval follows from producer-ingress green tests. Downstream independent review, promotion, retained-package loading and public projection must materialize and verify those bytes, bind semantic conflict/positive entailment, authorize each cell outcome and recognize `not_researched` before a release gate may pass. Until then, fail closed.
