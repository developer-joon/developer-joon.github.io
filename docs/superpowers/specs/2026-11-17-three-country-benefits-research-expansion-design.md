# Three-country benefits research expansion (design)

**Decision:** Research up to 10,000 distinct employing legal entities each in KR, US and JP as a long-term discovery ceiling. A candidate is not a published employer or a verified benefit. The owner chose a separate protocol requiring an official legal ID, current activity evidence, and employee-employment evidence; the original 2,000-entity v4 series remains byte-identical and governed by its original workforce, revenue, source and approval rules. No intermediate remote push, PR, merge, server, or deployment.

## Constraint and approaches

The v4 domestic revenue/assets thresholds and strict US SEC 10-K route cannot simply be expanded to 10,000 per country. The SEC reports 7,799 **all** reporting issuers in its 2025:Q3–2026:Q2 period, including foreign, shells and non-employers ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/statistics-data-visualizations/reporting-issuers)). DART offers a public company-overview Excel download with legal names and corporate registration numbers but no payroll field ([DART guide](https://dart.fss.or.kr/guide/main.jsp?menu=130)). The Japanese NTA offers a corporate-number bulk file with name/address/ID but no employees ([NTA](https://www.houjin-bangou.nta.go.jp/download/)). A registry row is not a benefit or payroll-employer assertion.

1. **Recommended: additive multi-registry research protocol.** In each jurisdiction stage official-ID *discovery* separately, then accept current active *employing* legal entities only after independently sourced activity and dated worker evidence. More effort and mixed identifier authorities, but truthful coverage and feasible beyond SEC alone.
2. Retain v4-only eligibility and sources. Stronger inherited comparability, but the US 10,000 target is unattainable through SEC issuers alone; not selected.
3. Publish 30,000 registry identities as employers immediately. Fast coverage, but includes inactive, non-employing and unrelated legal entities; rejected.

## Data flow and trust boundaries

- Source-specific private acquisition snapshots are hashed and recorded with retrieval time, authoritative endpoint, license/terms and permitted custody. Never retain unauthorized response bodies. DART bulk access is not itself republication permission; NTA attribution/processing disclosure does not authorize employer benefit-page text. SEC fair-access limits apply. No GitHub harvesting.
- Discovery inventory is append-only and versioned. Stable IDs: KR `KR-CRN-` + 13 digits; US `US-CIK` + ten digits only for SEC entities (other official US authorities require their own prefixed namespace and deduplication bridge); JP `JP-CN-` + NTA 13-digit corporate number. Never synthesize an HTTPS source URL, payroll link, or ID. Preserve aliases and predecessor/successor relationships without merging IDs by name. Check exact ID and normalized name against v4 and all expansion ledgers.
- A distinct `employment_verified` decision requires a source-dated current operation observation **and** a worker/employer observation explicitly attributable to the selected legal entity. A consolidated group headcount or group benefits page is only an unresolved lead. Missing or blocked evidence stays `research_incomplete` or `source_access_failed`, never `no_public_info` or “no benefits.” A separately assessed *recognition priority* may use attributed workforce, revenue, and listed/group prominence; it is an editorial processing order, not an objective national fame ranking. Never invent a missing priority score.
- Benefit observations remain private raw leads with exact URL, short observed span where permitted, conditions, geography and employment cohort. Public audit indexes are quote-free, credential-free HTTPS-only and explicitly `research_only`. Independently deduplicated, rights-cleared, current, employer-bound propositions alone can be promoted through a separately reviewed release gate. No 30,000-record live site projection until a sibling expansion artifact, browser loader and verifier all accept its exact inventory without changing the v4 freeze.

## Pilot, scale and stop gates

1. Before any 30,000-entity coverage claim, select a bounded **100-entity discovery assignment per jurisdiction** from an actually accessed official population (not necessarily 100 eligible employers). Capture exact IDs and order in one pinned manifest. In US, mix SEC issuers with a separately verified official non-SEC legal-entity route; do not call an EIN or ticker a payroll finding. In JP, the NTA sample is only a parser fixture: select current entries from a dated full release before accepting a candidate. KR DART bulk rows need dedupe and current-activity/worker joins.
2. Independently account for all 300 assigned IDs as identity-verified, employment-verified, unresolved, or excluded; 100 pilot rows are **not** a promise of 100 qualifying employers. Validate file SHA, exact set difference, country and entity scope, rights, source dates, and zero hidden/unpinned files. Record measured eligible yield, official data availability, retrieval effort, and review bottlenecks before claiming a feasible route to 10,000 each. If an official/non-SEC US employment bridge fails, stop at the proven cohort rather than padding the quota.
3. Expand only in disjoint, hash-pinned batches after the pilot, checking per-jurisdiction totals by set union, not raw benefit rows. Publish the full research corpus only after the separate authority/rights/UI gate and the user's one final feature-branch push; the owner handles PR, merge and deployment.

## Acceptance criteria for the first subproject

- Original 2,000 roster, 80 batch records, v4 protocol and prior 71 expansion identity candidates are byte-identical. No HTTP server or remote write.
- Three official-source path probes give a valid bounded input and an honest blocker when an employment link or reuse right is unavailable. The JP NTA sample is tested only as format, never counted as current companies.
- A versioned pilot contract and failing/passing tests reject duplicate/malformed IDs, wrong jurisdiction, stale/closed or insufficient activity/worker support, group-only employee counts, missing access/rights state, unsafe URLs, and unpinned or leaked private data. Record actual verified yield; no fabricated row to satisfy a quota.
- Output remains local/research-only until the full publication trust boundary is independently reviewable. Site claims do not imply this phase made a new benefit card available.
